# Product projection v0.2

The product verdict is a finite, ordered decision over fourteen separate layer results. Native replay, native semantic outcome and native adapter vocabularies remain visible. They are not Boolean aliases for the product result.

`profiles/product-projection-v2.json` is the machine-readable decision table. Its forty-six literal cases were specified independently of `projectV2`; expected outcomes are not generated by the function under test. `tests/projection-v2.test.mjs` checks the table and malformed boundaries, while `tests/generic-boundary-v2.test.mjs` and `tests/prf-composition-v2.test.mjs` exercise actual composed receipts and native execution.

| Priority | Condition | Product verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invalid layer inventory, field representation, status vocabulary, obligations or support flag | `REJECTED_MALFORMED` |
| 2 | Any admitted layer is `REJECTED_MALFORMED` | `REJECTED_MALFORMED` |
| 3 | Any layer contradicts/violates its relation, or native recomputation semantic outcome is `REFUTED` | `CONTRADICTED` |
| 4 | Unsupported profile or required adapter/semantic surface | `UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE` |
| 5 | Required computation cannot run/recompute or replay is `DIVERGED` | `CANNOT_RECOMPUTE` |
| 6 | Any required positive premise is unestablished | `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` |
| 7 | All preceding guards clear and all required successes are established | `VERIFIED` |

The six mandatory obligations always apply, even if `policy.required_layers` is empty:

| Layer | Required success |
| --- | --- |
| Integrity | `VERIFIED` |
| Authenticity | `VERIFIED` |
| Subject Binding | `VERIFIED` |
| Recomputation | `REPRODUCED`, with separate semantic outcome `VERIFIED` |
| Evidence Sufficiency | `SUFFICIENT` |
| Semantic Compatibility | `COMPATIBLE` |

Consumers can also require Guarantee Preservation (`PRESERVED`), Continuity (`PRESERVED` or `VERIFIED`) and Domain Adapter (`VERIFIED` or `PRESERVED`). Requested guarantees, continuity requests and the Interpoll trace adapter add their applicable obligation automatically. Omitted optional layers do not establish those guarantees. A supplied contradiction is never ignored merely because its layer was optional.

Integrity and authenticity success cannot override a subject, authority, scope, policy, version or dependency contradiction. `REPRODUCED` negative native evidence remains negative. An upstream native negative result can have a separately `PRESERVED` transfer relation; preserving that transfer does not make the original claim positive.

A PRF result retains its native tuple outcome, transfer status and separate
`productProjection`. When supplied, the nested product projection must contain
exactly `status` from the six-value product vocabulary and a `reasonCodes` array
of nonempty strings. Malformed or contradicted nested results take the same
precedence as other supplied layer results. For a requested PRF obligation,
unsupported, unavailable or insufficient nested product results also block
overall verification at their corresponding precedence. A successful transfer
status cannot hide these failures.

The surrounding RVR digest predicate and the separately caller-selected PRF
award/settlement obligation remain distinct relations. RVR may remain positive
while the PRF native tuple is negative and faithfully transferred. The report
then keeps RVR `VERIFIED`/`REPRODUCED`, PRF transfer `PRESERVED` and overall
`CONTRADICTED`; it does not relabel the transfer as `VIOLATED`. A PRF checker
crash or malformed native output yields overall `CANNOT_RECOMPUTE`, never a
semantic contradiction. Malformed PRF input is `REJECTED_MALFORMED` and an
unsupported PRF profile is `UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE`.

Package presence, a runner declaration, process exit zero, native runner `PASS`, backend-conformance `PASS`, and truthy JSON values are not positive semantic outcomes. An empty evidence collection cannot establish the mandatory evidence obligation. A crashed checker gives unavailable computation; it is not semantic refutation or mutation coverage. `DRIFT` is unavailable source reproduction rather than a semantic claim failure.

The projection proves only the declared composition of these input layers. It does not independently establish the soundness of caller-accepted dependencies, the real-world truth of signed declarations, scanner-origin execution, provider/hardware authenticity, general scope entailment, production security or ledger finality. Every `VERIFIED` result remains bounded by its displayed claim surface, accepted policy and adapter limitations.
